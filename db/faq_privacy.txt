Q: Who operates this platform? 
A: 
This platform is operated by the Franklin County Municipal Court Self-Help Center and Dispute Resolution Department. If you have any questions about how your personal data is handled, you may contact them directly at [mediation@franklincountymunicourt.org](mailto:mediation@franklincountymunicourt.org).  
 
Q: How is my personal information used?  
A: 
Personal information collected through this platform may be used to provide dispute resolution services. This includes scheduling mediation, communicating with users, and documenting mediation outcomes. Your information may also be used to help improve the system and inform future policy decisions. 
 
Q: Can I take screenshots of messages and/or Zoom meetings? 
No. You may not take screenshots of mediation messages or Zoom meetings. Digital communications in mediation are privileged. This means they generally cannot be shared or used in court, except in certain situations listed in O.R.C. § 2710.05. 
 
Q: Are my messages private in mediation? 
A:  
Mediation communications are generally protected, but there are exceptions under O.R.C. §2710.05. Mediators are required to keep any information learned in mediation private unless they are required or permitted by law to share it.  
 
A mediator **MAY** share a signed mediation agreement, or something said in mediation that is open to the public or part of a public record. A mediator **MAY** also share information about threats of bodily injury or violence, plans to attempt to commit, commit, or conceal a crime, child abuse, malpractice or professional misconduct by anybody participating in mediation. A mediator MAY also share something said in mediation in certain criminal proceedings, including some felony or juvenile cases, or when required by law to report knowledge of a felony. 
 
Mediation communications are generally privileged. This means that what is said in mediation generally cannot be used in a formal proceeding, such as in a court case. However, there are exceptions. For example, information may be used if the person with the privilege (1) allows it or (2) if an exception applies, such as child abuse, professional misconduct, or violent crimes. 
 
**Mediation is NOT always confidential.** This means that the other person in the mediation (not the mediator) may be able to share what happened in mediation with others, including friends, family members, social media, or news media. Do NOT share sensitive information that you are not comfortable with other people knowing or having access to. If you would like to make the mediation confidential, everyone participating must make a separate agreement.  
 
The mediator or the other person in mediation may not take screenshots of the conversation. 
 
 
Q: Who can see my personal information? 
A: 
Your personal information is shared only with the **Dispute Resolution Department** and the **mediator assigned to your case.**
 
Q: How long is my personal information kept? 
A: 
Your personal information is kept for one year, as required by the Franklin County Municipal Court.  
 
Q: Can I see or change my personal information? 
A: 
Yes. You may ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information at any time.  
 
You may ask the Court to stop using your information. However, this may impact your ability to use the mediation service.  
 
Q: How is my personal information protected? 
A: 
The platform uses security measures to protect your information. You should still be careful when sharing sensitive personal information online.
