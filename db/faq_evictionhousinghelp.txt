Q: What is the eviction process?
A: The eviction process usually has four steps: **notice, filing, a court hearing, and a judgment.**

**1. Notice**
	- Your landlord must give you a written **Notice to Leave the Premises** before filing an eviction.  
	- The notice explains why you are being asked to leave and when you must move out.  
	- A common example is a **3-day notice** for unpaid rent.  
**2. Filing an Eviction**
	- If you do not move out, your landlord may file an eviction case in court.  
	- You will receive a **summons** telling you when and where your court hearing will take place.  
**3. Court Hearing**
	- The hearing usually happens a few weeks after the case is filed.  
	- You and your landlord can explain your side, provide documents, and bring witnesses.  
	- You may want to speak with a lawyer before your hearing.  
**4. Judgement**
	- The judge will decide whether the landlord has the right to take back the property.  
	- If the landlord wins, the court may issue a **Writ of Restitution**, which allows the eviction process to move forward.  
	- If the tenant wins, the tenant may be able to stay in the home.  

For more step-by-step information about eviction and housing issues, visit [Ohio Legal Help](https://www.ohiolegalhelp.org/guide/housing).
