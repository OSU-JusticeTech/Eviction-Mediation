Q: How do I get started? 
A:  
Create a free account or log in if you already have one. Follow the steps to provide information about your rental problem through a short intake survey and invite the other person to join. 

Once both people have created accounts and agreed to participate, you can start negotiating through the chat. 

Q: How do I start a conversation with the other person? 
A:  
Once both people have joined the tool and agreed to participate, you can begin discussing the problem through text messages. 

Here are some tips to get started: 

    - **Explain the problem.** Share what happened and why it concerns you. 
    - **Listen to the other person.** Give them a chance to explain their side. 
    - **Ask questions.** Make sure you understand each other's concerns. 
    - **Discuss solutions.** Talk about what each person can do to solve the problem. 
    - **Be respectful.** Focus on finding a solution rather than blaming the other person. 

You do not have to agree on everything right away. If you need more help, you can request a mediator. 

Q: Do I have to be online at the same time as the other person? 
A:  
No. This tool works through text messages, so you can read and respond to messages when you have time. You do not have to be online at the same time. 
 
Q: When does a mediator join the conversation? 
A:  
You and the other person first try to solve the problem by talking directly to each other. If you need help reaching an agreement, you can request that a neutral mediator join the conversation. A mediator does not automatically join the chat. 

A mediator is a real person who helps both sides communicate and explore possible solutions. 

Q: What if the other person does not respond? 
A:  
Both people must participate for the conversation to move forward. If the other person does not respond, you may need to consider other ways to address the problem. 

Q: Can I use this tool on my phone? 
A:  
You can access the platform through a web browser on a phone, tablet, or computer.
