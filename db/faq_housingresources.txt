Q: Legal Information and Help
A:
**Legal Information and Help**
	- **[Ohio Legal Help](https://www.ohiolegalhelp.org/)**
	Free information about eviction, tenant rights, repairs, security deposits, and other housing problems. Includes forms and step-by-step guides.
	- **[Franklin County Municipal Court Self Help Center](https://www.franklincountymunicourt.org/Self-Help-Center)**
	Free help understanding court procedures, finding court forms, and learning about eviction and rent escrow. Staff can provide legal information but cannot give legal advice.
	- **[Legal Aid of Southeast and Central Ohio (LASCO)](https://www.lasco.org/apply)**
	Free legal help for people who qualify, including assistance with eviction and other housing problems.

Q: Rental and Financial Assistance
A:
	- **[Rentful614](https://www.rentful614.com/)**
	Information about rental assistance, eviction prevention, and local housing resources in Franklin County.

Q: Help With Other Housing Problems
A:
	- **[Franklin County Law Library — Tenant Resources](https://fclawlib.libguides.com/ohiolandlordtenantlaw/tenant)**
	Information about tenant rights, eviction defenses, rent escrow, and Ohio housing laws.
	- **[Ohio Legal Help — Repairs and Rent Escrow](https://www.ohiolegalhelp.org/topic/repairs)**
	Learn what to do if your landlord does not make needed repairs.
	- **[Ohio Legal Help — Fair Housing](https://www.ohiolegalhelp.org/topic/fair-housing-ohio)**
	Learn about housing discrimination and your rights under Ohio and federal law.
	- **[Ohio Legal Help — Utility Assistance](https://www.ohiolegalhelp.org/topic/utility-bill-assistance)**
	Find information about programs that may help pay gas, electric, and other utility bills.
